# Campfire - Executive Summary

## Company Overview

**Campfire** is an AI companion platform that enables users to create deeply personalized AI characters with unique personalities, voices, and visual identities. Unlike generic chatbots, Campfire companions remember conversations, build genuine relationships over time, and feel like real, persistent presences in users' lives.

---

## Mission

To create meaningful AI relationships that enrich human lives through personalization, persistence, and emotional intelligence.

## Vision

A world where everyone has access to supportive, understanding AI companions that adapt to their unique needs and grow alongside them.

---

## The Problem

### Current AI Assistants Fall Short

1. **Generic & Impersonal** - ChatGPT, Siri, and Alexa are utility-focused, lacking personality or emotional depth
2. **No Memory** - Conversations reset, requiring users to re-explain context every session
3. **Text-Only** - Limited to typing, missing the human connection of voice
4. **One-Size-Fits-All** - Cannot be customized to individual preferences or needs
5. **Loneliness Epidemic** - 1 in 3 adults report chronic loneliness; existing solutions don't address emotional connection

### Market Gap

Existing AI companion apps (Character.AI, Replika) offer limited customization, unreliable memory, and often feel shallow. Users want companions that truly know them.

---

## The Solution: Campfire

### Core Value Proposition

**Create AI companions that feel real** - with persistent memory, custom personalities, natural voice conversations, and dynamic visual identities.

### Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Personality-as-Code** | 12 archetypes + 10 personality sliders for precise character definition |
| **Persistent Memory** | Knowledge graphs and semantic memory that grow over time |
| **Voice-Native** | Real-time voice conversations with customizable AI voices |
| **Visual Identity** | AI-generated avatars with consistent appearance across sessions |
| **Relationship Pacing** | Companions evolve naturally, respecting user-defined boundaries |
| **Group Conversations** | Multiple companions interacting together |

### Technical Differentiators

- **Multi-LLM Architecture** - Anthropic Claude (primary) + OpenAI fallback ensures reliability
- **pgvector Memory** - Semantic search over all past conversations
- **ElevenLabs Integration** - Premium voice synthesis for natural speech
- **Event Sourcing** - Complete audit trail for debugging and analytics
