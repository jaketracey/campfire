This document presents financial projections for Campfire's first three years of operation. As a bootstrap/pre-seed company, these projections focus on capital efficiency, path to profitability, and funding requirements.

1. **Capital efficient model** - 67% gross margins enable sustainable growth

6. **Scalable cost structure** - Variable costs decrease with volume
